# Qualified Dutch B2B CV prospects

Research date: 5 August 2026  
Product considered: WerkCV Agency Plan — €149/month, up to 50 client-ready CVs, one branded workflow, Dutch-first formatting, PDF output, invoice billing.

## What “qualified” means here

These are not scraped company names. Each organisation below has a public Dutch website that shows at least one of the following:

- outplacement, re-integration, job coaching or career coaching as a real service;
- explicit CV/resume, motivation-letter or application coaching;
- a team, national coverage, multiple locations, group programmes or other signal that can create CVs repeatedly;
- a public business contact route.

The score is an outreach-priority score, not a claim that the organisation has already expressed buying intent.

- **Fit (0–5):** how directly the published service creates or improves client CVs.
- **Volume (0–3):** public evidence of repeat volume, team delivery, national coverage or group work.
- **Reach (0–2):** a clear public business contact route and a plausible owner of the workflow.

Before treating a lead as sales-qualified, confirm its monthly CV volume, current workflow, decision-maker, data-processing requirements and whether €149/month is within budget.

## Recommended execution

Do not email all 50 at once. Start with the first 15, split into three messages of five, and ask for a 15-minute workflow conversation. The first qualification question should be:

> “How many client CVs does your team create or materially revise in a typical month, and where does the work slow down today: intake, writing, formatting, review, approval or export?”

Advance a lead only when the answer confirms:

1. at least 10 recurring CVs/month or a clear upcoming cohort;
2. a named owner who can test the workflow;
3. no hard blocker around hosting, GDPR or client-data retention;
4. a real willingness to test the existing €149/month, 50-CV plan.

For the first outreach batch, prioritise **Amplooi, FourstaR, Nieuwe Sporen, CareerAdvisor, Werkcontact, Choice en Partners, RondomWerk, 2ndTRACK, Flexis, Werkraad, Carrièrepoort, MEPD, Nextjob, Bureau VolZin and Green Octopus**. Leave Right Management, USG Restart and GORT for a later enterprise/partnership motion even though their public volume signals are strong.

## Important limits

Public websites prove service fit, not buying intent. Team size, monthly CV volume, current software, budget, decision-maker and GDPR requirements still need to be confirmed in conversation. Public contact details can change, so verify the route immediately before sending.
