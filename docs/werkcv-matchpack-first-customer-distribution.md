# WerkCV MatchPack first-customer distribution runbook

**Campaign label:** `evidence_matrix_launch_2026_09`  
**Owner:** WerkCV founder/operator  
**Purpose:** a small, manual discovery effort for one external paying Agency customer.  
**Status:** owner-operated; no automated outreach, scraping or pilot funnel.

This runbook is deliberately practical and evidence-led. It does not promise a response, a sale or a search-engine citation. Use it only after the public guide, matrix downloads, free checker and Agency checkout have passed their release checks.

## 1. Select 20–30 suitable businesses

Create a private working sheet with one row per organisation and no candidate information. Record only the company name, public business URL, public business contact channel, fit notes, date checked, campaign status and aggregate outcome. Do not commit the sheet to the repository.

Select Dutch recruitment or secondment businesses whose public website shows most of the following:

- public-sector, IT or specialist professional recruitment/contracting;
- assignments with explicit requirements, knock-out criteria or requirement-by-requirement evidence;
- a public description of submitting or presenting candidates;
- a published business contact channel (general mailbox, contact form or company LinkedIn page).

Do not select a company solely because it appears in a search result. Read the public page and record the concrete fit before contacting it. Use public business channels only: never buy a list, scrape personal addresses, infer an employee email or upload a candidate CV.

Stop selecting when 20–30 organisations meet the rule. This is a bounded discovery set, not a claim about the size of the market.

## 2. Verify the destination before sending

Open these public routes in a private browser window and confirm that they work without an account:

1. `/voor-bureaus/kennisbank/kandidaat-aanbieden-overheid`
2. `/tools/kandidaatvoorstel-checker`
3. `/voor-bureaus/kennisbank/kandidaatvoorstel-voorbeeld`

Check that the guide has the Dutch answer, source-linked fictional example, matrix DOCX/CSV downloads and a clear limitation statement. Check that the free checker shows its complete result without email capture. Use only fictional data for a demonstration. Do not describe the protected claim verifier or candidate acknowledgement as active if its feature flag is off.

Use only bounded campaign parameters when useful, for example:

`?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=manual&utm_campaign=evidence_matrix_launch_2026_09&utm_content=guide`

Never put a recipient name, company name, email address, CV ID or other personal data in a URL or analytics field.

## 3. First message: lead with help, not a sale

Send one message through the organisation's published business channel. Do not ask for a meeting, subscription or pilot in the first message. Ask for one sentence of criticism after the recipient has a useful resource.

## 4. Follow-up and stop rules

Wait seven calendar days before one follow-up. Send it only when there was no reply, bounce or opt-out.

**Follow-up (Dutch):**

Hallo, ik stuur dit één keer na omdat de eisenmatrix misschien relevant is voor kandidaatvoorstellen met knock-out-eisen. Geen reactie nodig als het niet past. Als u één zwakke plek in uw huidige controleproces kunt noemen, helpt dat ons de gratis checker te verbeteren: https://werkcv.nl/voor-bureaus/kennisbank/kandidaat-aanbieden-overheid

Stop immediately when any of the following occurs:

- the organisation or person opts out;
- the message bounces or the channel is invalid;
- the recipient replies that the topic is not relevant;
- the recipient provides feedback (reply once, thank them, then do not continue the sequence);
- the organisation does not fit the selection rule after a closer check.

Never send more than the initial message and one follow-up to the same organisation in this campaign. Do not use urgency, false scarcity, discounts or a meeting request to force a response.

## 5. Record aggregate outcomes only

Use the existing privacy-safe analytics events and campaign label. Do not add free-form notes to analytics. The private working sheet may contain operational status but must not contain candidate data or personal profiling.

At campaign level, count:

- selected;
- contacted;
- delivered;
- replied;
- checker used;
- checkout started;
- settled paid Agency account;
- activated Agency account (MatchPack approved and approved PDF/DOCX exported).

The authoritative paid milestone is the settled subscription/payment record, including direct or unknown attribution. A browser success page, email open or checkout start is not a sale. Keep paid and activated counts separate.

Review the funnel after 7 and 14 days. Report raw counts and denominators; do not claim a conversion rate from a tiny sample. A reply is qualitative feedback, not proof of product-market fit.

## 6. Safety and privacy checklist

Before every send:

- public business channel verified;
- no candidate, client or vacancy data attached;
- no personal recipient data in URL parameters;
- message accurately describes the free checker and current feature flags;
- opt-out and stop rule understood;
- no claim of truth verification, identity verification, ranking, legal compliance or guaranteed client acceptance.

This campaign is manual distribution for learning and a first customer. It is not a bulk-email system, an automated lead list, a pilot funnel or a substitute for consent/legal review by the agency using WerkCV.
