# WerkCV Agency DPA review checklist

Status: **LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED — not an executed DPA**

This checklist is an internal preparation aid for the Agency/MatchPack release.
It is not legal advice, does not confirm GDPR/AVG compliance, and must not be
presented to a customer as a signed agreement.

## Facts to verify before accepting real candidate data

- WerkCV contracting entity, registered address and contact for controller/processor questions.
- Customer role and documented processing instructions for CVs, vacancy text, recruiter notes and exports.
- Exact production hosting provider, database location and backup location.
- Exact AI provider(s), model route, processing region, retention and training settings.
- Exact payment, login-email and monitoring providers used in the deployed path.
- Subprocessor list, contract dates, notification/change process and international-transfer mechanism.
- Security measures: access control, team roles, audit events, encryption, backups, deletion and incident response.
- Retention schedule and deletion receipt boundary for MatchPack content, exports, usage records and billing data.
- Data-subject request, breach-notification and assistance procedures.
- Whether a DPIA or additional controller/processor allocation is required for the customer’s use case.

## Customer-facing package to prepare

1. Executed DPA with the verified legal entity and processor list.
2. Technical and organisational measures (TOMs).
3. Current subprocessor annex and change-notification route.
4. Retention/deletion explanation linked to the deployed product behaviour.
5. Security and incident-contact route.
6. Evidence that provider facts and transfer arrangements were reviewed on the
   date the customer signs.

Until this checklist is completed and legally reviewed, public copy should say
that DPA and subprocessor status require verification and should direct agencies
to use fictional or properly authorised data for testing.
